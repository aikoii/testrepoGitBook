# Remarkably Bright Creatures

## Remarkably Bright Creatures

_Remarkably Bright Creatures_ is a novel by Shelby Van Pelt.

The story follows Tova Sullivan, a night cleaner at an aquarium. After loss reshapes her life, she forms an unlikely bond with Marcellus, a perceptive giant Pacific octopus.

### Why it resonates

* Explores grief with warmth and humor.
* Connects people through quiet acts of care.
* Gives Marcellus a distinctive, memorable voice.

Published in 2022, the novel blends mystery, family history, and an unexpected friendship.
